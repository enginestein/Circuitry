# EFI Module Harvest — L450 / AIVL1 (R06ET69W)

Raw carve of the 8 MB firmware image `600260` for PE32/PE32+ images.

- **376 unique PE32+ (x64) modules** recovered (MZ→`PE\0\0`, machine 0x8664), plus one
  32-bit (0x14c) module seen in the raw scan that the dedupe dropped (offset 0x611e0, small
  legacy/PEI intruder).
- Carves live in `codeGetExtractPath/_thinkpad_bios.bin.extracted/efi_pe/mNNN.efi`
  (binary, indexed by the manifest order in `efi_manifest.txt`).
- Structured per-module classification: `efi_catalog.json`.
- `m000.efi` (@ 0x1094, 91 KB) is the DXE core (`DxeMain`), consistent with the
  sector `module_4244.exe` found earlier (@ 0x4244).

## Vendor fingerprints

- **Lenovo**: 6 modules (mostly `nvram`-keyword, i.e. Lenovo BIOS NVRAM/variable tooling).
- AMI/Insyde strings: none found → confirmed Phoenix platform (matches EC `Phoenix` tag).
